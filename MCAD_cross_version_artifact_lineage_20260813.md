## Critical artifact audit findings

1. The legacy `article_update`/figure layer is byte-identical across multiple July/August snapshots; it was not refreshed to represent the later robustness and sensitivity program.
2. `experiments/article/artifacts/generate_article_artifacts.py` explicitly implements `figure_bootstrap_proxy` and `figure_ablation_proxy`; its `scalability_latency_vs_nvs.png` is based on campaign latency rows rather than the recovered structural-scalability benchmark, and its `evidence_usefulness_summary.png` represents A/B/C physical ALLOW/BLOCK counts rather than the recovered Phase-6 evidence-usefulness benchmark.
3. The same generator falls back to technical A/B/C validation checks for `human_validation_scores.png` when a real human-validation report is absent. It must not be used as expert/human evidence.
4. The reworked deliverable adds `table_expert_annotation_protocol.tex`; the protocol can be documented, but simulated/empty-label material does not authorize human agreement/F1 claims.
5. Therefore the final article must regenerate its final figures/tables from qualified campaign data, while retaining old figures/tables only as historical editorial references.

## Recommended final separation

- `experiments/article/campaigns/`: scientific evidence hub (campaigns, provenance, recovered sources, qualification).
- `article_update/artifact_history/`: optional history/index of old article artifact packages; do not use as numerical authority.
- `article_update/paper_artifacts_final/`: only final normalized data, statistics, figures, tables, claims, sections, checksums and publication manifests.
